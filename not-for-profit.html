The mission-performance architecture

The MD recommends presenting services as an integrated operating architecture, not isolated boxes. Courses should teach that same model.

TAG

Systems, talent, performance

Courses on operating rhythms, decision visibility, staff and volunteer capability, service delivery, and measurement.

BIG

Financial stewardship

Courses on mission ROI, program economics, funding readiness, budget visibility, risk, and board reporting.

AIM

Stakeholder clarity

Courses on funder communication, impact narrative, digital presence, donor/member confidence, and community trust.

Course categories

These categories come directly from the not-for-profit pathway recommendation and translate TAG's commercial architecture into mission-oriented language.

Governance & Board Clarity

Board roles, oversight, meeting rhythm, decision rights, and accountability.

Mission ROI & Stewardship

Program economics, resource allocation, cash-flow visibility, and evidence of impact.

Back-of-House as a Service

Ongoing operating support across systems, finance, talent, and communications.

Systems & Workflow Automation

Capacity recovery through better tools, documentation, data flow, and automation.

People, Volunteers & Culture

Staff, volunteer, leadership, partner, and board capability continuity.

Funding & Funder Confidence

Grant readiness, reporting discipline, funder materials, and trust-building evidence.

Stakeholder Communication

Community, member, donor, partner, and funder-facing clarity.

Capacity Assessment

Diagnostics across governance, systems, finance, people, data, and confidence.
